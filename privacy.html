Privacy Policy

Preflop Reps · effective September 23, 2026

Preflop Reps does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • On-device only. Your drill history, streak and progress are stored on your device and never leave it.
  • No analytics or tracking. The app contains no third-party code that reports on you, and it does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive no personal information from a purchase; Apple's privacy policy governs that transaction.
  • Aggregate statistics. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may share aggregate, anonymized install and crash statistics with us.

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
