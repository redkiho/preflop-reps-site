Privacy Policy

Preflop Reps for iPhone and iPad · updated November 20, 2026

Preflop Reps is published by Kiho Hong. The sections below explain which reporting practices apply to the version installed on your device.

Version 1.0 (build 19)

Version 1.0 (build 19) keeps drill history, progress and streaks on your device. It sends no usage reports to the developer. Your device backup may include this information. We cannot access that backup.

Version 1.0.1 and usage reporting

In version 1.0.1 (build 22), first-party usage reporting starts on the first welcome screen when the reporting service is enabled. We use it to find where players leave onboarding and where purchases or restores fail, and to measure how often players return.

Your choice

Open Privacy settings on the welcome screen, use the gear during onboarding, or open Settings from Today. Under Privacy, turn off Share usage data. This cancels queued sharing and stops new reports. You can keep playing, purchasing and restoring. If you enable reporting again, it starts with current activity. The app does not send activity from the time sharing was off.

What we receive

A report may contain the app build, a UTC calendar day, the onboarding steps reached, the shop entry point, product or entitlement category, and the outcome of a purchase or restore. A random reference lets us count updates to a single visit once. A reference belongs to that visit alone.

Separate activity reports count app-use days and returns on days 1, 7 and 30 after reporting begins. Each event has its own short-lived retry reference. We do not include names, emails, advertising or device identifiers, Apple transaction IDs, payment details, your poker answers, or your saved drill history in these reports.

Hosting and use

The reporting service uses Cloudflare to receive HTTPS requests, limit repeated requests and store the measurements. Cloudflare handles network information, including the connecting IP address, to deliver and protect the service. Our reporting database does not store IP addresses or user-agent strings. We do not use reports for advertising, combine them with third-party identities or sell them.

Retention

Visit-level records expire from the active database after up to 45 calendar days. Activity retry records expire after up to four calendar days. Non-identifying daily counts may remain. On our Cloudflare Free plan, database recovery copies can retain expired records for up to seven additional days. Turning reporting off does not retract a report that has already reached the service.

Apple services and local data

Both versions run without an account or ads. Apple processes purchases. Optional reminders are scheduled on your device. App Store Connect provides the developer with aggregate store activity and sales figures. Some usage and diagnostic figures depend on your separate Apple analytics-sharing choice.

Contact

For privacy questions or a request about your data, use the support link below. GitHub issues are public. Please leave payment details, receipts and other private information out of the issue; you can ask to arrange a private follow-up. Our usage reports carry no permanent player identifier.
