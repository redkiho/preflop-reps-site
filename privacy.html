Privacy Policy

Preflop Reps for iPhone, iPad and Android · effective November 1, 2026

Preflop Reps does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • On-device only. Your drill history, streak and progress are stored on your device and never reach us. If your device backs up to iCloud or to your Google account, that backup may include them; we have no access to it.
  • No analytics or tracking. The app contains no third-party code that reports on you, and it does not track you across apps or websites. It shows no ads.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store, or by Google through Google Play. We receive no personal information from a purchase; the store's privacy policy governs that transaction.
  • Reminders. The optional daily reminder is a notification scheduled on your device.
  • Aggregate statistics. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, or usage and diagnostics data with Google on Android, Apple or Google may share aggregate, anonymized install and crash statistics with us.

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on the device. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
