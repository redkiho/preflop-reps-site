Sori Support

Sori helps you practice how written Korean words are pronounced on iPhone and iPad.

Get help

Contact the developer on GitHub about a problem or to suggest a change. Include your Sori version from Settings and what happened. For a pronunciation question, include the word and the chapter.

GitHub requires a sign-in to post. Support requests are public. Keep personal information and purchase receipts out of your post.

Restore Sori Full

On Today, open Sori Full and tap Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that bought Sori Full. For a refund, go to Apple’s Report a Problem.

Manage reminders and audio

Open Settings from Today to turn reminders, automatic pronunciation, or set completion sounds on or off. Words with a speaker button can still be played on demand.

Word list: National Institute of Korean Language, 한국어 학습용 어휘 목록 (2003), KOGL type 1.
