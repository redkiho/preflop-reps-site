Privacy Policy

Updated November 20, 2026

Practice and progress

Sori keeps your placement, practice answers, streak, and preferences on your device. Those records stay in the app’s storage during use. The developer receives none of them. The app has no accounts, ads, tracking, analytics service, or microphone recording. It uses the device’s Korean voice to play pronunciations.

Purchases and reminders

Apple processes Sori Full purchases. Sori checks the purchase status supplied by the App Store to unlock or restore Full. The developer does not receive your payment details. Sori schedules optional reminders on your device. You can turn them off in Sori’s Settings or in your device’s notification settings.

Keeping and removing your data

Deleting Sori removes practice history and preferences from the app’s storage on that device. Apple manages device backups and your App Store purchase history separately.

Contacting support

GitHub hosts the support site and public issue tracker. If you post a support request there, GitHub handles the information you provide under its privacy statement. The developer uses your post to respond and investigate issues. You can edit or request removal of the issue through GitHub. Keep personal information and receipts out of public posts.
