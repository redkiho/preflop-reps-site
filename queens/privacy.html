♛ Queens Rematch

Privacy Policy

Updated November 27, 2026

Puzzles and progress

Queens Rematch keeps your boards, lessons, solve times, streak and preferences on your device. The developer receives none of them. The app has no accounts, ads, tracking or analytics service, and it does not connect to the developer’s servers. Every puzzle is built into the app.

Purchases and reminders

Apple processes the archive purchase. Queens Rematch checks the purchase status supplied by the App Store to unlock or restore the archive. The developer does not receive your payment details. The app schedules optional reminders on your device. You can turn them off in the app’s Settings or in your device’s notification settings.

Keeping and removing your data

Deleting Queens Rematch removes your progress and preferences from the app’s storage on that device. Apple manages device backups and your App Store purchase history separately.

Contacting support

GitHub hosts the support site and public issue tracker. If you post a support request there, GitHub handles the information you provide under its privacy statement. The developer uses your post to respond and investigate issues. You can edit or request removal of the issue through GitHub. Keep personal information and receipts out of public posts.
