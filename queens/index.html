♛ Queens Rematch

Queens Rematch Support

Queens Rematch is a daily logic puzzle for iPhone and iPad. Place one crown in every row, column and coloured region, with no two crowns touching. When a crown fools you, the board shows why, and a smaller rematch built on the same trap lets you beat it.

Get help

Contact the developer on GitHub about a problem or to suggest a change. Include your app version and what happened. For a puzzle question, include the date of the daily or the name of the board.

GitHub requires a sign-in to post. Support requests are public. Keep personal information and purchase receipts out of your post.

Restore the archive

Open the archive (the books at the top of the board) or Settings (the gear) and tap Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that bought the archive. For a refund, go to Apple’s Report a Problem.

Reminders, sound and letters

Open Settings from the gear at the top of the board to turn the daily reminder, sound effects, the region letters or the tap-to-mark cycle on or off.
