Each spot is at a nine-handed table with 100 big blinds. Answers are the app's standard ranges, not solved ones, built from published charts, some of them six-handed. A spot is in the app only when at least three independent publishers agree on it: similar width, with the hands at the edge within about one notch. They are good defaults for a typical low-stakes live game, not the last word against every opponent. How we grade

Your own open is 4 big blinds; a raise you face is shown at 3. Those are the app's sizes for a live game.
